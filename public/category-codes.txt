Looters Computas — title stamps
First letter of the category + a digit that looks like the 2nd letter.
Put ONE stamp as its own word in the Trade Me title.

L4   Laptops       (LA)
D3   Desktops      (DE)
C0   Components    (CO)
M0   Monitors      (MO)
S7   Storage       (ST)
P3   Peripherals   (PE)  keyboards, mice, headsets
N3   Networking    (NE)

Examples
  L4 HP EliteBook 840 G5 i5 8GB
  D3 HP ProDesk 400 G4 SFF
  C0 Cooler Master 212 CPU cooler
  M0 Dell 24" LCD
  S7 Samsung 1TB NVMe
  P3 Attack Shark X3
  N3 TP-Link AX1800

Graphics (GR) and Accessories (AC) have no digit that looks like the
2nd letter — stamp those as C0 with components until you pick a code.

Exact stamps only. HP G4/G5 and Apple M1 are NOT category codes.
