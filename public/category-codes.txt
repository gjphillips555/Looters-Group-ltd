Looters Computas — title category stamps
Put ONE stamp as its own word in the Trade Me title (start or end is fine).
The number can be 0–99. Brackets optional: L4  or  [L4]

L4   Laptops        /shop/laptops
D3   Desktops       /shop/desktops
C0   Components     /shop/components
MN0  Monitors       /shop/monitors
AX0  Accessories    /shop/accessories
GC0  Graphics       /shop/graphics
ST0  Storage        /shop/storage
KB0  Peripherals    /shop/peripherals   (keyboards, mice, headsets)
NT0  Networking     /shop/networking

Examples
  L4 HP EliteBook 840 G5 i5 8GB
  D3 HP ProDesk 400 G4 SFF
  C0 Cooler Master 212 CPU cooler
  MN0 Dell 24" LCD
  AX0 USB-C hub 7-in-1
  GC0 NVIDIA GTX 1660 Super
  ST0 Samsung 1TB NVMe
  KB0 Attack Shark X3
  NT0 TP-Link AX1800

Do not use G4 / G5 / M1 as stamps — those are already in PC model names.
Use GC0 for graphics and MN0 for monitors instead.
