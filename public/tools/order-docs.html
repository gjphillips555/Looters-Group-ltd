Consumer Guarantees Notice
Refurbished / Second-life goods – New Zealand

Your statutory rights (Consumer Guarantees Act 1993)

These goods are sold as refurbished or second-life. The Consumer Guarantees Act still applies. The goods must be:

  • Of acceptable quality – taking into account their age, price, and that they are used/refurbished. They must be safe, durable for a reasonable time given their condition, and free from defects that a reasonable buyer would not expect.
  • Fit for purpose – suitable for the purposes goods of that type are commonly used for, and any particular purpose you told us about.
  • Match their description – as advertised or described to you.

If the goods fail any of these guarantees you are entitled to a remedy from us (repair, replacement, or refund). These rights cannot be contracted out of or reduced by any private terms.

This is a summary of the minimum legal requirements only. Full details: Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 and consumerprotection.govt.nz.
